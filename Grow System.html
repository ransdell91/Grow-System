Dual Layer Method — Why It Works
🧬
Happy Frog on top = gentle, microbe-rich zone seedlings start in. Ocean Forest below = hot, nutrient-dense zone mature roots tap into. The plant self-selects the right zone at each life stage.

🔄 Re-Amendment Between Seasons

After harvest, your soil is depleted but the biology is established. Re-amend instead of replacing:

  1. Remove top 2" of old soil (root debris, old top dress)
  2. Mix in 3 tbsp / gal of 4-4-4 through the remaining soil
  3. Add 10–15% fresh worm castings by volume
  4. Top with 2" fresh Happy Frog
  5. Water in with Recharge 3 days before planting

Complete Feeding Timeline
⏱️ Dosing by Stage — Per Gallon of Soil
⚠️
Ocean Forest is pre-charged for 4–6 weeks. Do NOT add 4-4-4 at transplant. Wait for plant to show early hunger signs (slight lightening of lower leaves) before first top dress. Happy Frog is gentler — light feeding can start at week 3.
Week 0–2 — Seedling
No amendments. Plain pH'd water at 6.3–6.5. FFOF and HF have enough. Let the soil biology establish.
Recharge at ½ tsp/gal is fine from week 1
Week 3–4 — Early Veg
First top dress if showing hunger: 1 tbsp 4-4-4 per gallon of soil. Work lightly into top ½" and water in. Add ¼ cup worm castings on top.
Signs of hunger: yellowing lower leaves, slow node spacing
Week 5–6 — Peak Veg
Second top dress: 1–1.5 tbsp 4-4-4 per gallon. Plants are vigorous now and can handle more. Continue Recharge weekly.
This is your big veg push — support it with adequate nitrogen
Week 7 — Pre-Flip (1 week before 12/12)
Transition top dress: 50/50 blend — 1 tbsp 4-4-4 + 1 tbsp 2-8-4 per gallon. Signals the shift without abruptly dropping nitrogen during stretch.
Do this top dress 7–10 days before flipping the light schedule
Week 8–9 — Early Flower / Stretch
Switch to pure bloom: 1 tbsp 2-8-4 per gallon. This feeds the stretch and early bud site formation. Continue worm castings.
Watch for nitrogen excess — deep green, clawing leaves = back off N
Week 10–11 — Mid Flower
Final top dress: 1 tbsp 2-8-4 per gallon. This is your last amendment — needs 4–5 weeks to fully break down before harvest.
Do not top dress after this point — excess N in late flower = harsh smoke
Week 12–14 — Late Flower
Water only. pH 6.3–6.5. Let the soil fully feed the plant from the breakdown happening below. Recharge can continue through week 13.
Trichomes going cloudy → amber. Watch closely with a loupe
Week 14–16 — Final Flush (Optional)
If using organic dry amendments and good biology, a true flush is optional — the microbes naturally deplete the soil. If flushing: 2–3 plain waterings before harvest.
2-day dark period → harvest → hang at 60–65°F / 55–62% RH

💧 Watering Protocol
  • Always pH to 6.3–6.5 before watering
  • Water when top 1–2" of soil is dry — lift the pot; lightweight = water time
  • Water to 10–15% runoff each session to prevent salt buildup
  • Let soil dry slightly between waterings — wet/dry cycles strengthen roots
  • Add Recharge at ½ tsp/gal every 7–10 days (not every watering)
  • Never water seedlings to runoff — small amounts in a ring around the stem
